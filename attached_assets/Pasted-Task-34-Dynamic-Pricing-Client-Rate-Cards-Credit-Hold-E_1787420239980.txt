Task #34 — Dynamic Pricing, Client Rate Cards & Credit Hold Engine

Objective

Build the internal commercial engine for Marcus Sessions.

Do not connect Stripe or move real money yet.

The system must support:

Client has prepaid/granted training value
→ Client books
→ Maximum personal 1-to-1 amount is held
→ Session takes place
→ Marcus records attendance
→ Final class size is known
→ Each attendee is charged their own applicable group rate
→ Excess hold is released

No-show charges are decided separately by Marcus.

⸻

Approved Product Rules

These rules are approved and should be treated as the source of truth.

1. Dynamic class pricing

Price depends on the number of clients who actually attend.

Example only:

Attendees	Standard rate
1	€40
2	€35
3	€30
4	€25

Actual prices must be configurable by Marcus.

⸻

2. Individual client pricing

Different clients may have different prices.

Support:

* Marcus default pricing
* Pricing tiers such as:
    * Standard
    * Legacy
    * Friends & Family
* Individual client-specific pricing

Example:

Standard client 1-to-1: €40
Legacy client 1-to-1: €25

Both can attend the same session and be charged different amounts.

⸻

3. Pricing hierarchy

Use:

Client-specific pricing
↓
Assigned pricing tier
↓
Marcus default pricing

A specific client override always wins.

⸻

4. Pricing Snapshot at Booking

When a booking request is created, determine the client’s applicable pricing.

Snapshot the complete applicable rate card onto the commercial booking record.

Example:

1 attendee → €40
2 attendees → €35
3 attendees → €30
4 attendees → €25

This snapshot must not change if Marcus edits prices later.

Historical/current bookings must continue using the pricing that applied when they booked.

⸻

5. Maximum Hold

When the client books, reserve their personal maximum possible amount.

The maximum is their:

1-to-1 rate

Example:

Standard client
1-to-1 = €40
→ hold €40
Legacy client
1-to-1 = €25
→ hold €25

The hold is not a charge.

It temporarily reduces the amount available for another booking.

⸻

6. Insufficient Balance

A client cannot create a booking if they do not have enough available training balance to cover their maximum hold.

Example:

Balance: €30
Required maximum hold: €40
→ booking cannot proceed

Return a clear client-facing state explaining that insufficient training balance is available.

Do not create the booking and then discover the balance problem afterwards.

⸻

7. Pending / Confirmed Booking

The maximum hold remains active while the booking is:

pending
confirmed

Marcus confirming the booking must not create another hold.

One booking = one active hold.

Retries must not double-reserve value.

⸻

8. Rejected Booking

If Marcus rejects the request:

full hold released

No value is consumed.

⸻

9. Rescheduling

Rescheduling must not create a second commercial reservation.

The original hold should transfer/follow the replacement booking.

If the applicable pricing snapshot remains the same, reuse it.

Do not:

old booking hold
+
new booking hold

and accidentally reserve twice.

⸻

10. Cancellation

A dedicated late-cancellation fee policy has not yet been defined.

For this phase:

cancelled booking
→ full remaining hold released

Do not implement cancellation penalties in Task #34.

This can be changed later through an explicit cancellation-policy task.

⸻

11. Final Pricing Uses Attendance

The current pricing rule is:

Final class price is based on the number of people who actually attend.

Example:

4 booked
3 attended
1 no-show

Pricing participant count:

3

The three attendees are each charged their own snapshotted 3-person rate.

The no-show does not count toward the attendee pricing tier.

⸻

12. Different Rates in the Same Session

Example:

Three people attend.

Their snapshots may contain:

Client A 3-person rate = €30
Client B 3-person rate = €30
Legacy Client 3-person rate = €20

Settlement:

Client A → €30
Client B → €30
Legacy Client → €20

Do not force all participants to share one price.

⸻

13. Excess Hold Release

Example:

Client:

Maximum hold = €40
Final 3-person price = €30

Settlement:

€30 consumed
€10 released

The final charge may be lower than the hold.

It must never exceed the original hold.

⸻

14. No-Show Rule

No-show pricing is manually controlled by Marcus.

When Marcus marks a client as No-show, he must be able to choose:

Waive

Charge = €0
Full hold released

Charge amount

Marcus selects an amount:

€0 → maximum original hold

Example:

Original hold: €40
Marcus may charge:
€10
€20
€25
€40
etc.

The selected amount must never exceed the original hold.

⸻

15. No-Show Audit

Persist:

* original held amount
* selected charge
* amount released
* waived yes/no
* actor
* timestamp
* optional note/reason

No-show financial decisions must be auditable.

⸻

16. Settlement

Do not charge attendees before final attendance is known.

Create a clear settlement step after attendance outcomes have been recorded.

Preferred Marcus workflow:

Session ends
↓
Marcus records Attended / No-show for each participant
↓
No-show charge decisions completed
↓
Marcus reviews settlement
↓
Settle Session

Settlement preview should show:

Client	Outcome	Held	Final charge	Release
Client A	Attended	€40	€30	€10
Client B	Attended	€40	€30	€10
Legacy Client	Attended	€25	€20	€5
Client D	No-show	€40	Marcus choice	remainder

Do not settle while unresolved active participants remain.

⸻

17. Settlement Idempotency

Settlement is financially important.

Repeated requests must never:

* charge twice
* release twice
* duplicate ledger entries
* duplicate no-show charges

The same settlement retry must resolve to the same financial result.

⸻

18. Internal Balance Model

Do not use a mutable integer such as:

credits = 5

The commercial value varies by client and rate.

Use value-denominated accounting.

Store EUR amounts in integer minor units:

€40.00 = 4000
€25.00 = 2500

No floating-point financial calculations.

⸻

19. Ledger

Use an auditable ledger for actual balance movements.

Examples:

+12000  manual/package value grant
-3000   attended session settlement
-2000   no-show charge
+5000   Marcus manual grant

A hold is a reservation, not a completed ledger charge.

Conceptually:

Ledger balance
-
active holds
=
available balance

confirmed participant count

* clients see only their own balance/pricing outcome
* clients cannot edit pricing
* clients cannot grant balance
* Marcus/admin can manage pricing according to capabilities
* tenant isolation
* one client can never see another client’s custom rate

Do not rely on frontend hiding.
