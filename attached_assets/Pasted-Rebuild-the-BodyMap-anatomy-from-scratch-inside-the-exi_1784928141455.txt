The result should feel like a polished fitness app body map, not a medical diagram and not a stick figure.

- Full upper arms
- Elbows
- Forearms
- Hands
- Defined torso
- Pelvis
- Thighs
- Knees

- Ankles
- Feet
- Front and back views

## Important

Do not use one silhouette with floating muscle polygons placed over it.

Instead, construct the body from coordinated anatomical regions so every muscle shape follows the body structure precisely.

All paths must share the same coordinate system and align cleanly.

Use a viewBox of:

0 0 240 520

Create separate front and back SVG definitions.
