Replit Prompt — Sessions & Booking Gate 1 Inspection

You are the Builder for the Marcus Grima Fitness / Grima Fitness Hub project.

Follow the Universal Product & App Build Operating System as the governing framework.

Task

Perform a full inspection only of the existing Sessions & Booking feature.

Do not implement, modify, migrate, refactor, publish, or change production or development data.

This is an AMBER inspection gate only.

Objective

Determine exactly what exists today, what is hard-coded or simulated, what persists, what depends on other systems, and what is missing before Sessions & Booking can become a production-ready vertical feature.

The future architecture must support different pricing per individual client, without hard-coding client-specific prices directly into booking logic.

Architecture principle

Treat booking, pricing, and payment/entitlement as related but separate concerns.

A booking should not permanently depend on a single fixed pricing model.

The eventual system should be able to resolve the price/entitlement applicable to a specific client while preserving the price or entitlement state used at the time of booking.

Do not design a full solution yet. Identify what the current codebase would require to support this safely.
