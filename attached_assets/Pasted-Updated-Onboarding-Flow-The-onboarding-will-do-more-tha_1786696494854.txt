Updated Onboarding Flow

The onboarding will do more than collect information. It will build the client profile, understand their goals, assess their starting point and immediately turn that information into a clear plan and projected goal timeline.

The principle is:

Understand the person → understand the goal → understand the starting point → build the plan → show the destination.

1. Welcome

A short introduction from Marcus explaining what the app will help the client do:

Train with a clear plan
Stay accountable
Track progress
Build better habits
Stay connected to Marcus

Primary action:

Start my journey

2. About You

Collect the basic information needed to build the client profile:

Name
Date of birth
Gender, where relevant to coaching
Height
Current weight
Preferred units
Occupation
Typical daily activity level
3. What Are You Working Towards?

Clients select their primary goal.

Examples:

Lose body fat
Build muscle
Get stronger
Improve fitness
Improve mobility
Build consistency
Improve sports performance
Return to training
Feel healthier
Other

Then ask:

What would success look like for you?

This gives Marcus context beyond a generic fitness goal.

4. Why Does It Matter?

The client explains the motivation behind the goal.

Questions could include:

Why do you want to achieve this now?
What would achieving this change for you?
Is there a specific date or event you are working towards?

This information becomes part of the client’s motivation profile and can later support accountability and coaching.

5. Your Starting Point

Understand the client’s current training experience:

Beginner / Intermediate / Experienced
Current training frequency
Previous training experience
Current sports or activities
Gym access
Equipment available
Preferred training days
Typical session length
6. Health & Safety

Collect information relevant to safe programming:

Injuries
Current pain
Previous surgeries
Relevant medical conditions
Relevant medication
Movement limitations
Pregnancy where applicable
Anything else Marcus should know

Any important health or injury information is clearly flagged in Marcus HQ before the programme is assigned.

7. Lifestyle

Understand what the programme needs to fit around:

Sleep
Stress
Work schedule
Daily activity
Average steps
Travel
Recovery
Alcohol
Smoking
Available training time

This helps Marcus build something realistic around the client’s actual life.

8. Nutrition

Build an initial picture of the client’s eating habits and challenges:

Main nutrition goal
Typical number of meals
Dietary preferences
Allergies
Foods avoided
Current calorie tracking
Water intake
Supplements
Biggest nutrition challenge

Possible challenges:

Portion control
Snacking
Weekends
Eating out
Meal preparation
Protein intake
Consistency
Not knowing what to eat
9. Your Weekly Routine

Clients identify when training can realistically fit into their week.

For each day:

Preferred
Available
Not available

This gives Marcus a practical starting point for programme design.

10. Baseline

Create the starting point for future progress.

Depending on the coaching service, this can include:

Weight
Measurements
Progress photos
Strength benchmarks
Fitness tests
Daily step average
Resting heart rate
Personal bests

This becomes the baseline against which future progress is measured.

11. Coaching Preferences

Understand how the client responds best to coaching.

Examples:

Keep me accountable
Push me hard
Encourage me
Give me more detail
Keep things simple
Explain the reasoning
Check in with me regularly

Also ask:

When you are struggling, what usually helps you get back on track?

12. Goal Forecast

This is the key moment in the onboarding journey.

Using the information collected during onboarding, the app will calculate a realistic projected timeline for reaching the client’s goal.

The forecast can use inputs such as:

Starting point
Target
Training frequency
Activity level
Lifestyle
Nutrition approach
Previous experience
Expected consistency

The client immediately sees something like:

Your Goal
Lose 8 kg

Estimated Timeline
16–20 weeks

Projected Goal Date
18 December 2026

Your Starting Plan
3 training sessions per week
8,000 daily steps
Nutrition target
Weekly check-in

The forecast is presented as an estimate, not a guarantee.

Your projected date will update as your progress, habits and consistency change.

13. Review & Commit

The app summarises the onboarding information into a clear starting plan.

For example:

Your goal
Lose 8 kg and improve overall fitness

Your timeline
Approximately 16–20 weeks

Your training
3 sessions per week

Your focus
Strength, nutrition consistency and daily movement

Your projected goal date
18 December 2026

Primary action:

I’m ready to start

This gives the onboarding a clear sense of commitment and completion.

14. What Happens Next?

The client sees exactly what happens after onboarding.

For example:

Marcus has everything he needs to get you started.

Profile complete ✓
Assessment complete ✓
Goal forecast created ✓
Programme being prepared
First session pending

From here, the client can:

Book their first session
Message Marcus
Explore educational content
Set up notifications
Complete their first habit
15. First-Day Home Screen

The first home screen should immediately turn the onboarding into action.

For example:

Good morning, Sarah. Here’s your next step.

94 days to your goal

Today:

Complete your workout
Hit your water target
Reach your step goal
Log your nutrition
Message Marcus if you need help

Status: On Track

This connects everyday behaviour directly to the client’s long-term goal.

Marcus HQ During Onboarding

Marcus sees the same onboarding data from the coaching side.

For each client, Marcus can view:

Goal
Motivation
Current fitness level
Training availability
Health flags
Lifestyle factors
Nutrition challenges
Coaching preferences
Baseline measurements
Progress photos
Projected goal date
Onboarding completion status

Example:

Sarah Borg — 100% onboarding complete

Goal: Lose 8 kg
Training: 3x weekly
Experience: Intermediate
Injury flag: Knee discomfort
Nutrition challenge: Weekends
Coaching preference: High accountability
Projected goal date: 18 December
Programme: Not assigned

Primary Marcus action:

Create Programme

The result is that onboarding immediately becomes part of the coaching workflow, rather than simply becoming stored questionnaire data.
