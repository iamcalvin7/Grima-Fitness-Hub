Redesign the Body Map component from scratch. The current version is too simplistic and looks like a placeholder. I want it to match the quality and style of the reference image while keeping my brand colours.

## Goal

Create a premium, anatomically inspired body map that feels modern, realistic and polished. The body should look closer to a real human silhouette rather than a basic mannequin.

The design should feel similar to Apple Health, Whoop, Levels or high-end fitness apps.

## Visual Style

- Dark premium interface
- Clean vector illustration
- Anatomically accurate muscle groups
- Thin light-grey outlines
- Soft rounded curves
- Symmetrical proportions
- Elegant, minimal aesthetic
- High contrast
- Premium mobile app quality

Avoid cartoon or blocky body shapes.

## Body

Replace the current stick/mannequin figure with a realistic athletic silhouette.

Requirements:
- Better head proportions
- Defined neck
- Natural shoulders
- Realistic torso width
- Defined waist
- Proper arm proportions
- Proper leg proportions
- Hands and feet visible
- Smooth vector curves
- Front and back views

The finished body map should look like something found in a premium fitness application, not a simple illustration.
