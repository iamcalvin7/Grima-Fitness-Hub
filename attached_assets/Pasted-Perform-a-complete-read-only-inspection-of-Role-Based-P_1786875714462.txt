Perform a complete, read-only inspection of Role-Based Permissions in Grima Fitness Hub.

## Approved v1 product scope

The product has exactly two actors:

1. **Client** — a member using the app for coaching, programmes, workouts, bookings, progress, nutrition, content and communication.
2. **Marcus** — the sole operator. Marcus combines trainer and administrator responsibilities.

There must be no separate Trainer or Admin product roles in v1.

Internally, Marcus’s permissions should remain divided into two explicit capability groups:

* **Coaching capabilities:** client management, programmes, assignments, bookings, attendance, progress, feedback and communication.
* **Administrative capabilities:** content, memberships, payments, credits, offers, configuration and sensitive operational actions.

Marcus receives both capability groups. This separation must be preserved so additional staff roles could be introduced later without rewriting the permission system.
