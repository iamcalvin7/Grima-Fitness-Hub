REDESIGN THE MARCUS GRIMA COACHING LANDING PAGE — MOBILE FIRST

Goal:
Redesign the existing Marcus Grima coaching landing page so it feels calmer, more premium, more credible, and more aligned with the new brand positioning.

The current version is cleaner than before, but still feels too much like a conventional fitness landing page.

The new direction should feel closer to a premium wellbeing / performance brand.

Think:
- calm
- breathable
- human
- premium
- credible
- editorial
- personal
- minimal

Not:
- aggressive gym marketing
- SaaS
- sales funnel
- dark bodybuilding aesthetic
- feature-heavy
- overly corporate
- cluttered

The supplied mockup/reference is the visual direction.

IMPORTANT:
Build MOBILE FIRST.

The majority of traffic will come directly from Instagram, so the mobile experience is the primary experience.

Desktop should expand elegantly from the mobile system, not the other way around.

--------------------------------------------------
BEFORE CHANGING ANYTHING
--------------------------------------------------

1. Locate the current Marcus coaching landing page and all components/styles it uses.
2. Read the relevant files fully.
3. Preserve the existing WhatsApp destination exactly.
4. Reuse existing components where appropriate.
5. Do not change auth, database, backend, routes, environment variables, API behaviour, or unrelated pages.
6. Do not install new dependencies unless absolutely necessary.
7. Do not publish.

--------------------------------------------------
CORE BRAND POSITIONING
--------------------------------------------------

Marcus is not the hero of the client's story.

The client owns their goals and their life journey.

Marcus provides the guidance, structure and support that helps them move towards those goals.

Primary brand line:

YOUR GOALS.
MY GUIDANCE.

Secondary philosophy:

OWN YOUR JOURNEY.

Marcus now represents three interconnected areas:

Physical Training
Mental Wellbeing
Life Coaching

The overall experience should communicate that Marcus is much more than someone who writes workout plans.

--------------------------------------------------
VISUAL DIRECTION
--------------------------------------------------

Move away from the current mostly black/white visual system for this landing page.

Introduce a calm premium palette inspired by the reference:

- warm white / very pale grey backgrounds
- soft sky blue
- muted blue-grey
- deep navy / charcoal typography
- natural photography
- subtle translucent surfaces
- green reserved primarily for WhatsApp / conversion actions

The blue should create a feeling of:
space
air
calm
clarity
wellbeing

Do not make it look like a medical website or generic tech startup.

Avoid bright saturated blue.

Use soft, natural tones.

--------------------------------------------------
PHOTOGRAPHY
--------------------------------------------------

Photography should become a major part of the page.

Marcus himself provides credibility.

Use the existing Marcus photography available in the project wherever possible.

Photography should feel:
- premium
- natural
- cinematic
- aspirational without looking staged
- human

Ideally show different sides of Marcus:

1. Strong hero portrait
2. Physical coaching/training
3. Thoughtful / conversational / reflective moment
4. Lifestyle / life coaching context

Do not use generic stock photography if appropriate Marcus imagery already exists.

Do not AI-generate new people.

--------------------------------------------------
MOBILE PAGE STRUCTURE
--------------------------------------------------

Keep the page SHORT.

Target approximately 2–3 natural mobile scrolls.

Do not build a long landing page.

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1 — HERO
--------------------------------------------------

The hero should be visually led by Marcus photography.

Mobile structure:

FULL-WIDTH MARCUS IMAGE
then content directly beneath or partially overlapping depending on what works best.

Eyebrow:
MALTA · ONLINE & IN-PERSON

Headline:
YOUR GOALS.
MY GUIDANCE.

Supporting copy:
Personalised coaching to help you become stronger, healthier & happier.

Then show the three areas compactly:

Physical Training
Mental Wellbeing
Life Coaching

Do not use emoji here in the redesigned version.

Use small clean line icons or simple typographic treatment consistent with the site's design system.

Primary CTA:
LET'S TALK ON WHATSAPP →

Microcopy:
Free introduction. No commitment.

The CTA should use the existing WhatsApp destination.

On mobile:
- strong full-width CTA
- generous touch target
- no tiny buttons
- no unnecessary sticky CTA obscuring content

The hero should immediately answer:

Who is Marcus?
How can he help me?
What should I do next?

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2 — COACHING BUILT AROUND YOU
--------------------------------------------------

Eyebrow:
PERSONALISED COACHING

Headline:
COACHING BUILT AROUND YOU.

Supporting line:
Because your goals, your life and your starting point are different.

Replace the current text-heavy numbered rows with THREE highly visual compact cards.

Each card should use photography.

CARD 1:
TRAIN

A physical plan built around you.

CARD 2:
THINK

Guidance for confidence, mindset and mental wellbeing.

CARD 3:
LIVE

Support beyond the gym, for the life you want to build.

Mobile:
Use either:
- horizontal swipe cards
OR
- compact stacked photographic cards

Choose whichever gives the cleanest mobile experience.

Do not make each card excessively tall.

The photography should carry much of the storytelling.

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 3 — CREDIBILITY
--------------------------------------------------

Add one very restrained credibility strip.

Do not invent any statistics.

If verified data already exists in the project, it may be used.

Potential examples ONLY if verified:

10+ Years Coaching Experience
Clients Across Malta & Internationally
Online & In-Person

If exact claims cannot be verified, use non-numerical proof instead:

PERSONALISED COACHING
DIRECT ACCESS TO MARCUS
ONLINE & IN-PERSON

This section should feel like supporting evidence, not advertising.

Keep it compact.

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 4 — FINAL BRAND MOMENT
--------------------------------------------------

Use another strong Marcus image.

Eyebrow:
MY PHILOSOPHY

Headline:
OWN YOUR JOURNEY.

Copy:
Your goals are yours.
My role is to help you move towards them.

CTA:
TALK TO MARCUS →

Use the same WhatsApp destination.

This should feel like the emotional conclusion of the page rather than another sales block.

--------------------------------------------------
COPY PRINCIPLES
--------------------------------------------------

Keep copy extremely concise.

Avoid:
- long paragraphs
- coaching clichés
- aggressive motivation
- “results not excuses”
- “transform your life”
- fake urgency
- overexplaining
- unnecessary features
- generic performance jargon

The page should feel like Marcus speaking directly to one person.

--------------------------------------------------
DESIGN DETAILS
--------------------------------------------------

Mobile first.

Recommended principles:

- 16–20px mobile body copy where appropriate
- strong headline hierarchy
- generous but controlled whitespace
- max readable line length
- rounded image/card corners
- subtle translucent or frosted UI elements where useful
- very light borders
- soft shadows only if needed
- avoid heavy card containers everywhere
- avoid excessive icons
- avoid visual noise

Use whitespace and photography instead of adding UI.

The page should feel breathable.

--------------------------------------------------
RESPONSIVE BEHAVIOUR
--------------------------------------------------

Build from mobile upwards.

Check at minimum:

375px
390px
430px
768px
desktop

On mobile:
- no horizontal scrolling
- no clipped headlines
- CTA remains easy to tap
- cards stay readable
- photography crops intentionally
- text never overlays important parts of Marcus's face/body
- no giant desktop spacing carried into mobile

Desktop can become more editorial:
- split layouts
- larger photography
- three cards side by side
- wider credibility strip

But maintain the same visual language.

--------------------------------------------------
WHAT TO REMOVE / REPLACE
--------------------------------------------------

Remove or replace:

- the current white-only functional presentation
- numbered feature rows
- unnecessary repeated explanation
- any remaining hard-performance messaging
- multiple repetitive CTA areas
- excessive borders
- anything that makes the page look like SaaS
- emoji-based service presentation
- generic “Ready to start?” ending

Do not reintroduce the old long landing page.

--------------------------------------------------
ACCESSIBILITY
--------------------------------------------------

Maintain WCAG AA contrast.

Because we are introducing pale blue / grey backgrounds:

- normal text minimum 4.5:1
- large text minimum 3:1
- UI controls minimum 3:1

Do not use pale grey text that becomes difficult to read.

Buttons must have clearly visible focus and hover states.

--------------------------------------------------
FINAL QA
--------------------------------------------------

After implementation:

1. Run TypeScript/typecheck across the relevant workspace.
2. Zero TypeScript errors.
3. Confirm the WhatsApp links still use the existing destination.
4. Check mobile at 375px, 390px and 430px.
5. Check desktop.
6. Confirm no horizontal overflow.
7. Confirm image crops are intentional.
8. Confirm WCAG AA contrast.
9. Confirm no old landing-page sections remain hidden or duplicated.
10. Confirm no unrelated routes/components were changed.
11. Provide a concise list of files modified.

Do not publish.

Return the redesigned page for review first.
