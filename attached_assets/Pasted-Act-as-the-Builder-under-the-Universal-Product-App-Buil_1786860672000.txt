## 3. Trace both sides of every feature

For each client-facing capability, verify whether the required Marcus HQ, trainer or admin counterpart exists.

Trace the full loop where applicable:

1. Operator creates or configures it.
2. Data persists.
3. The correct client receives it.
4. The client uses or completes it.
5. The result persists after refresh.
6. Marcus can review the outcome.
7. Notifications and analytics are triggered.
8. Permissions are enforced server-side.
9. Empty, loading, error and retry states are handled.
10. Tests prove the workflow.

A polished screen without this operational loop must not receive a production-ready score.

## 4. Score every feature out of 100

Score against world-class, production-ready quality, not visual completeness.

Use these dimensions:

* UX/UI quality and responsiveness: 15 points.
* Frontend workflow completeness: 10 points.
* Backend/API implementation: 15 points.
* Persistence and data integrity: 15 points.
* Marcus HQ/operator counterpart: 10 points.
* Authentication, permissions, privacy and security: 10 points.
* Empty, loading, error, retry and edge-case handling: 5 points.
* Automated tests and verified workflows: 10 points.
* Integrations and environment readiness: 5 points.
* Documentation and dependency clarity: 5 points.

If a dimension is genuinely not applicable, mark it N/A and normalise the score across the applicable dimensions. Explain every N/A.

Use this interpretation:

* 0–20: Concept or visual demo only.
* 21–40: Partial implementation with major workflow or backend gaps.
* 41–60: Core path exists, but operational, testing or edge-case gaps remain.
* 61–80: Strong working feature requiring production hardening or depth.
* 81–95: Production-ready or nearly ready.
* 96–100: Verified world-class end-to-end implementation.

Never inflate a score because the interface looks polished.
